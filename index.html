OUR HERITAGE · 62 YEARS OF TRUST

About HSI Solutions

For sixty-two years, HSI Solutions (Hospital Services, Inc.) has dedicated itself to simplifying the complexities of healthcare operations for community and critical access providers throughout the Upper Midwest.

Founded in 1963 as a wholly owned subsidiary of the North Dakota Hospital Association (NDHA), our organization was created with a clear imperative: to ensure that independent and rural hospitals could access the scale, purchasing leverage, and specialized operational acumen typically reserved for major metropolitan hospital conglomerates.

We believe deeply that operational efficiency must never come at the expense of clinical excellence or hometown trust. In community healthcare, every patient is a neighbor, a family member, or a colleague. That is why our collections process is founded upon dignity, transparent reporting, and patient advocacy.

Through our long-term strategic alliance with Vizient, we channel Tier-1 national group purchasing contracts into the hands of local hospital administrators, delivering aggressive supply savings that are reinvested directly into patient bedside care and state-of-the-art diagnostic technology.

Governed by a Board of Directors comprised of active, visionary hospital CEOs across North Dakota and South Dakota, HSI Solutions remains firmly grounded in the realities of regional healthcare delivery.
